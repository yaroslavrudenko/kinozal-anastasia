# Performance tracking — «Настин кинозал» (Nastin Kinozal)

## The governing rule: performance in balance with up-to-date information (owner, 2026-10-27)

Every performance change in this file is judged by this rule first. Verbatim:

> «Нужно сохранить баланс между производительностью и достоверностью информации, я хочу видеть актуальные списки каналов и групп, видеть что реально идёт по каналам — те, что в эфире, с разумной задержкой. В прошлый раз мы ради улучшения производительности поломали вообще состояния эфира. Также я хочу, чтобы стримы загружались максимально быстро и не подвисали, не тупили и не замерзали. Чтобы эффективно подгружался контент и не было раздражающей буферизации.»

> «То есть избыточного кеширования тоже не должно быть.»

> On buffering, corrected by the owner: «Я не говорил делать стримы без буферизации — я имел в виду, что пользователь не должен видеть постоянно экран с буферизацией на нём, с крутящимся прогрессом; сама буферизация, возможно, нужна и важна».

In English: keep the balance between performance and accurate information. The channel and group lists are up to date, and what is on air is shown with a reasonable delay; last time a performance change broke the on-air state, which must not happen again. Streams start as fast as possible and do not hang, stall or freeze; content loads efficiently, and the viewer does not keep seeing the buffering screen with its spinning progress (the buffering itself may be needed and important, and stays). No excessive caching either.

What this means for every change:
- **No performance change makes the lists, the groups or the «now» lines less fresh.** No refresh interval is lengthened (the list's 1 h, the `NOW_*` cadence, the open menu's 60 s re-read, the guide), no scheduled download is skipped, and no on-air line is served from a cache longer than before. A change that touches a refresh path carries a test that pins its freshness.
- **No new cache, and no existing cache lives longer or covers more.** The offline copy of the list is a fallback for a failed or slow download and a first paint only; the fresh list always downloads at boot and replaces it.
  - **Exception, channel logos (DEC-2026-10-28-logos-page-plus-next, the owner's order of 2026-10-28: «Возможно, стоит иконки кешировать … кэшировать так же, как кэшируются каналы»):** a logo loaded once is kept in memory for the page's life (at most `LOGO_KEEP` = 400 pictures) and never requested again while the list still names its URL; each hourly list forgets the URLs it no longer has, a changed URL is a new one. Nothing is persisted (no localStorage, no data URIs); across restarts the browser's own HTTP cache answers. A logo that failed (or was not answered within `LOGO_TIMEOUT_MS` = 8000 ms) is asked for again once per hourly list, never within the hour (DEC-2026-10-28-logo-retry-hourly, fix batch 5). Since fix batch 7 (DEC-2026-10-28-logos-ahead) the zap group's logos (≤ `LOGO_GROUP_MAX` = 400) are also loaded into that map in the background after the first picture — into memory only, never into the rows of a hidden screen.
- **The stream path is changed only with evidence of a faster start or less visible buffering**, never by shrinking the buffers.

**Decision 2026-10-27: the channel list and its groups refresh every hour** (was 6 h), for every provider — OTT Club, iptv-org, one's own. Owner, verbatim: «Нужно сделать раз в час». `CONFIG.PLAYLIST_REFRESH_MS = 3600 * 1000`; retries, «a refresh held back is retried», and a fresh list replacing the offline copy are unchanged. The download happens every hour, and every list downloaded is parsed and applied. (Review 4, 2026-10-27, removed the P-3 skip of a playlist with the same `Last-Modified` and length: Codex reproduced a list rewritten at the same length under the same `Last-Modified` kept stale — the owner's rule, «актуальные списки каналов и групп», «избыточного кеширования тоже не должно быть», decides; see the review-4 rows below.) Cost, measured with the harness (`roundtrips.js`): +1 list request per hour of playback (OTT Club 445 KB, iptv-org's whole list 2.57 MB); with the menu open, also the one immediate fresh «now» re-read after a catalog that comes from the provider's cache (60 → 62 requests in an open-menu hour). The «now» lines are never older than before: across the hour no «now» source is more than 61 s from the next (`tests/refresh-now.test.js`).

## Summary of the 2026 performance work

### How it started

On 2026-10-25 six independent reviewers read the whole code of v1.7.0 (HEAD `20c4c6e`): two frontend/JavaScript code reviewers, two performance specialists, a general code reviewer and Codex as an external reviewer. Together they reported **230 findings**. All six verdicts were **REJECT**; none of the findings was critical. Performance came first. Every ◀ ▶ press downloaded a 205 KB guide. The channel list was parsed twice and written to storage before the first frame. A failed guide request was retried in a tight loop. A background health check of every channel competed with playback. The details are in [section 3](#3-baseline--2026-10-25-review) and the [backlog](#4-performance-backlog).

### How it was done

- **A fix plan that disposed of every finding.** Each of the 230 findings got a status: **115 FIX**, **112 MERGED** into a primary that the same work fixes, and **3 OPEN-NO-CLEAN-FIX** because of provider or platform limits. No finding was dropped.
- **One implementer, one batch.** The 724-line `app.js` was split into small single-purpose modules. A single transport module now owns every request, and every request can be aborted.
- **Three independent reviewers after every iteration:** the native frontend code reviewer, Codex (external, read-only) and the performance analyst. None of them wrote code. The performance analyst re-ran the measurements with scripts of its own rather than trusting the implementer's numbers.

### What the viewer notices

- **Instant zapping.** A single press opens the channel at once. In a burst of presses only the channel you stop on loads its guide, and the channels you skipped past are cancelled.
- **Pause stays paused.** Neither the watchdog nor the background check nor a pending zap overrides a pause. A pause shorter than 2 minutes resumes in place.
- **The archive resumes in place.** A reconnect or a return from Home reopens the recording at the position on screen, not at its start. The position follows the player's clock, so time spent buffering is not counted as watched.
- **No truncated text.** Channel names and programme titles wrap instead of being cut off. A row taller than the screen can be scrolled through line by line.

- **A stream tech line on every channel open:** resolution, codec, bitrate and sound appear with the banner for 5 seconds. It never shows the previous channel's values.
- **Paging never skips channels.** CH± / PG± turn the page by the rows that were completely visible, so every channel appears fully on some page.
- **A new name:** the app is now called «Настин кинозал» (Nastin Kinozal).
- **No black screen after another app:** returning from Home / another app reopens the channel (verified on the TV).

### «Now» lines: the refresh policy (DEC-2026-10-26-now-refresh-2, 2026-10-26)

Decided 3/3 by the native, Codex and business judges (`COMMON-DECISION-Q1`); it replaces the 60 s floor and the «any channel's end» trigger of DEC-2026-10-26-now-refresh. Code: `js/nowlines.js` (one governor), `js/epg.js` (store and lookups), `js/config.js` (numbers).

- **Only the rows on screen create network demand.** A row whose programme ended since the last fresh answer was *asked for* (its send time, so an answer that crossed the end does not count) is due at once; a row the provider still named after its end is re-asked 60 s (`NOW_RECHECK_MS`) after that answer; a row it had nothing for, after 5 min (`NOW_MISSING_MS`). The plain launch list is not a fresh answer. Ends of shows off screen send nothing.
- **A row whose next programme is known locally** (a full or kept guide, e.g. the playing channel) turns over at its end by a redraw alone: no request, not delayed by the floor.
- **Every refresh asks the provider-fresh list** (`?t=<seconds>`); the plain URL serves a cache minutes old. One request, parsed at idle. D6b (a cached launch list re-read at once) is unchanged.
- **Gates (one governor, no second path):** 10 s from the previous answer or failure to the next request (`NOW_MIN_INTERVAL_MS`), 1 s without a redraw or key (`NOW_QUIET_MS`), 5 s after a channel starts, never during a zap burst, a list load or in the background. While a menu or search is open the list is also re-read 60 s after the last attempt (`NOW_OPEN_REREAD_MS`; was 5 min until the owner's 2026-10-26 «accuracy over the saving»; best-effort under the same gates). Correction 2026-10-27 (P-11): this line said 5 min after the code had moved to 60 s.
- **Nothing is lost.** An answer that leaves a channel out keeps that channel's line (merge); a failed, empty or unusable answer changes nothing and is retried 60 s later, never faster and never stopping; only a channel that leaves the catalog loses its line. A record that has started is never replaced by one that has not (the provider's gap).
- **What a row shows** (menu, search and banner share `EPG.shown` / `EPG.label`): the programme on air, plain, including the 15-min linger after its end; past the linger, the last programme the provider named, marked «Название (шла до HH:MM)» with its own end time — never blank, never claimed as on air. The banner shows the same programme and mark with its time range (progress capped at 100 % by `UI.banner`). The ▼ panel's «в эфире», the refresh scheduling and the archive (D1) do not use the fallback.

## 1. Purpose

The player has one performance goal: films never stall and zapping is fast on a 2017 Samsung TV (UE50MU7000, Tizen 3.0, Chromium 47 web engine, Wi‑Fi). This file is the single place that tracks every known performance issue, its status, and every change made for performance, with before/after numbers. A performance change is not done until it has a row in the [change log](#6-change-log) and its backlog item points to the commit.

## 2. How we measure

**Rule:** every change records a before/after taken with a named command or procedure. "Faster" without a number is not accepted. Each number states where it was taken: **TV** (measured on the device), **desktop** (measured on a Mac), or **estimate** (not measured).

|---|---|---|---|

| Stalls over 3 movie channels × 10 min | 0 stalls · 0 rebuffers · 0 reconnects · buffer never below 12 s | desktop | parallel soak, 3 × 10 min |

### Measurement procedures

- **TV timings:** connect the Tizen debug inspector to the installed app and read timestamps from the console / network panel. Record the TV model, firmware and Wi‑Fi conditions with the result.
- **Request counts:** count requests in the inspector's network panel for a fixed key sequence (for example, 10 presses of ▶ in a row).
- **Soak:** play the stated channels for the stated time and record stalls, rebuffers, reconnects and minimum buffer level.
